Questions, answered.

Grouped by who’s asking. If yours isn’t here, the code and the full security notes are on GitHub.

For traders

Do I really need no data at the stall?

Checking a receipt needs none. Your phone needs data once to set up, and now and then to refresh the price and the mint’s keys, collect money and cash out. The app always shows how old its saved rate is.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Sealpay opens in the browser and can be added to your home screen. After the first visit it opens with no data.

What if I lose my phone?

Your recovery code brings your shop key to a new phone. If you saved a backup file, add it too: it brings back your money and your list of scanned receipts. Without the PIN, someone holding your old phone can’t spend what you collected.

How do I get the money into naira or CFA?

Cash out to your own Lightning wallet. Some Lightning wallets pay out to a bank in naira or CFA; Sealpay doesn’t handle that step.

Can two of my phones share one shop?

They can, but each phone only knows the receipts it scanned itself. A receipt shown on both could pass twice. For now, one phone per shop is safest.

What does it cost?

Sealpay is free. The mint may charge a small fee when you collect; the customer’s page adds it to their payment so you get the full price.

For customers

Which wallet do I need?

Any wallet that pays a Lightning invoice. You don’t need a Cashu wallet or a new app.

Can I show a screenshot of my receipt?

The trader’s phone reads the code, so a screenshot of a real, unused receipt works. A screenshot of one already accepted says Already used.

What if I pay and the page closes?

The payment is saved before the invoice appears. Open the page again and it finishes on its own.

About the money

What is a mint?

A service that holds bitcoin and issues Cashu ecash for it, like a market cashier who hands out signed tokens. Sealpay checks the mint’s signature; the mint holds the money until the trader cashes out.

Is this real money?

The demo and the test mint use fake sats. A trader can choose a real mint for real money. Keep small amounts there and cash out often.

Can a scammer fake a green screen?

Not on your phone. The green screen comes from your phone’s own check of the mint’s signature. A scammer can show a fake screen on their phone, which is why Sealpay never asks you to trust theirs.

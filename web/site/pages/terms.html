Sealpay is early software, offered as is, with no warranty.

Demo and test mint
Fake sats only. Nothing there is real money.
Real money
A Cashu mint holds the bitcoin until you cash out to your own Lightning wallet. If the mint fails or cheats, that money can be lost. Use a mint you trust, keep small amounts there and cash out often.
Your recovery code
The only way to get your shop key back on a new phone. Write it down and keep it safe. Nobody can reset it for you.
The check
Tells you a receipt was signed by your mint, is locked to you and was not used on this phone before. It can’t stop a mint from failing later.

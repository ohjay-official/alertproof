For traders and POS agents.

Know the money is real before the goods leave your hand, even when your data is finished.

What you need

  • An Android phone or iPhone with a camera and a browser. No app store download.
  • Data once, for about a minute, to set up. After that the app opens and checks receipts with none.
  • A pen and paper for your recovery code.

Setting up

  1. Name your shop

    The name customers see when they pay. Choose naira or CFA, and English or French for the Paid and Not paid screens.

  2. Choose a mint

    The mint holds the money until you cash out. Use the test mint to try Sealpay with fake sats, or a real mint for real money.

  3. Write down your recovery code

    28 characters in groups of four. It brings your shop key to a new phone. Keep it on paper and never share it.

  4. Choose a PIN

    4 to 8 digits. You need it to collect, cash out and save a backup. Checking payments never asks for it.

A day at the stall

Morning, with data
Open Sealpay once. It saves today’s rate and the mint’s keys, and finishes anything left from yesterday.
Each sale
Type the price, show the pay code, scan the customer’s receipt. Green means release the goods. Red means don’t.
Part payments
If a receipt is real but short, Sealpay keeps it, tells you how much more to ask for, and makes a pay code for the rest.
Evening
Open Sales to see what you sold, collect it with your PIN, and share a plain summary to WhatsApp if you like.
Cashing out
Make an invoice in your own Lightning wallet and paste it in. Sealpay pays it and keeps any change for you.

Keeping your money safe

Collected money stays locked to your key, so someone holding your phone without the PIN can’t spend it. Wrong PINs slow down after the third try.

Save a backup file from Sales now and then. It holds your money and your list of scanned receipts, but not your key: to restore it, you also need your recovery code.

What it costs

Sealpay itself is free. The mint may charge a small fee when money is collected; the customer’s page adds it to what they pay, so the full price lands with you. Your own wallet may charge to pay out to a bank.

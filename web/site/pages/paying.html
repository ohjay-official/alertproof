Paying a Sealpay trader.

No new app, no sign-up. You pay from the wallet you already use and get a receipt the trader’s phone can check.

Four steps

  1. Scan the trader’s code

    Use your phone’s camera. Sealpay’s pay page opens in your browser with the shop’s name and the price.

  2. Check the price

    The page compares the naira or CFA price on the code with the sats it asks for, at today’s rate. If they don’t match, it warns you before you pay.

  3. Pay the invoice

    Tap Open my wallet, or copy the invoice into any Lightning wallet, and pay.

  4. Show your receipt

    The page shows a receipt as a QR code that plays in parts. Hold it up until the trader’s phone says Paid.

If something goes wrong

The signal drops
Your payment is saved before the invoice appears. Open the page again and it picks up where it stopped.
You change your mind
Tap I’m not paying this. The page keeps a record for a day, so if a payment lands after all, it comes back.
A dispute later
Your receipt stays on your phone for a week, so you have proof as well.
The trader says Not paid
Only a receipt the mint really signed, locked to that trader, passes. A screenshot of a receipt you already showed says Already used.

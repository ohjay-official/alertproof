How it works

  1. Type the price in naira or CFA and show your pay code.
  2. The customer scans it and pays from their own Lightning wallet. They get a receipt.
  3. You scan their receipt. Your phone checks it by itself, with no internet.

Why a fake can't pass

  • The receipt is signed by the mint. An edited amount or a made-up receipt fails the check.
  • The money is locked to your key. The customer can't take it back or spend it elsewhere.
  • Your phone remembers every receipt. Showing one twice says "Already used".
  • Only your own phone's check counts. A screenshot or an alert on the customer's phone means nothing.

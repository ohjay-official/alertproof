For market traders and POS agents

Payments a fake alert can’t fake.

The customer pays from any Lightning wallet and shows you a receipt. You scan it, and your own phone says Paid or Not paid in under a second. With no data.

Try the demo See how it works

The demo runs on fake sats. One phone plays both the trader and the customer.

A credit alert is only a message.

At the stall, a sale closes when the customer shows a screen. Fake alert apps print convincing ones, screenshots are easy to edit, and on a bad network even the real alert arrives late. In a Punch survey of 330 traders in four Lagos markets, 52% had received a fake alert at least once.

Source: Punch, February 15, 2025.

Today

  • You trust whatever is on the customer’s screen.
  • You wait for your own alert, and the queue waits with you.
  • If it never comes, the goods are already gone.

With Sealpay

  • Only your own phone’s check counts.
  • It reads the mint’s seal on the receipt, with no internet.
  • Edited, copied or made-up receipts turn red before the goods move.

One scan, three answers.

The whole screen changes colour, the phone speaks the result and buzzes, so a trader with busy hands knows without reading.

How a sale goes

  1. Type the price

    In naira or CFA, on a keypad that works like a POS terminal. Your phone shows a pay code.

  2. The customer pays

    They scan the code and pay from the Lightning wallet they already use. Their phone gets a receipt.

  3. You scan the receipt

    Your phone checks it by itself and says Paid or Not paid. Collect the money later, when you have data.

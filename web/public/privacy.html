Sealpay

Privacy

Sealpay has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own.

What stays on your phone

Your shop name, your key (encrypted with your PIN), your saved mint keys and price, and your sales. They are saved in your browser on this phone only. Nobody else can see them, including us.

Who your phone talks to

  • The mint you choose, to make payments, collect and cash out. It sees those requests, as any Cashu mint does. It never learns your shop name.
  • CoinGecko, to get the price of bitcoin in naira and CFA.
  • Vercel, which hosts these pages. Like any web host it may keep basic request logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.

Checking a receipt sends nothing anywhere.

Camera

Sealpay uses the camera only on the scan screen, to read the receipt code. Pictures never leave your phone.

Cookies

None. The site sets no cookies, and a check runs after every update to make sure it never does.

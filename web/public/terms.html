Sealpay

Terms

Sealpay was built for Dakar Bitcoin Hack 2026. It is early software, offered as is, with no warranty.

  • Demo and test mint: fake sats only. Nothing there is real money.
  • Real money: a Cashu mint holds the bitcoin until you cash out to your own Lightning wallet. If the mint fails or cheats, that money can be lost. Use a mint you trust, keep small amounts there and cash out often.
  • Your recovery code is the only way to get your money back on a new phone. Write it down and keep it safe. Nobody can reset it for you.
  • The check tells you a receipt was signed by your mint, is locked to you and was not used on this phone before. It can not stop a mint from failing later.

The code is open source under the MIT licence: github.com/ohjay-official/sealpay.

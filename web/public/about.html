Why Sealpay exists.

A fake alert costs a trader the goods and the day’s profit. The fix shouldn’t need the network that keeps failing them.

The idea

At a Lagos stall, the proof of payment is a message on the customer’s phone, and the trader’s own alert depends on a network that drops at the worst time. Sealpay moves the proof into the receipt itself, so the trader’s phone can check it on the spot.

It started in November 2026, built on Cashu, the open ecash protocol, because Cashu receipts carry a signature a phone can check with no internet.

Who builds it

Sealpay is built by Oh-Jay, a Nigerian Web3 writer and builder, with Claude writing code, tests and docs under his direction. Every product decision is his.

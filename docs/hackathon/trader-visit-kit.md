# Trader visits: kit for Nov 17

Goal: 5 traders or POS agents near school. Their own words go in the README and the pitch, only with their permission, never their full names unless they agree.

## Before you go

- Open sealpay-beta.vercel.app/app#demo on your phone with data, once. It then works with no data.
- Charge the phone. Turn the volume up so they hear "Paid".

## Say first (in your own words)

"I'm a student building an app that helps traders check a payment is real before giving goods, even with no data. Can I ask you five quick questions and show you? I won't use your name unless you say I can."

## The five questions

1. How do you confirm a transfer today before you release goods?
2. Have you, or someone you know, lost goods to a fake alert or an edited screenshot? What happened?
3. How much data do you buy in a week, and how often does it run out or the network fail?
4. Which phone do you use? (brand and model, or show you)
5. If a customer showed you a receipt that your phone checks with no data, and the money lands in your bank or wallet at the end of the day, would you accept it? What would worry you?

## Then show the demo

Type their usual price, show the pay code, tap **Demo: pay it as the customer**, then **Switch network off and check it**. Watch where they hesitate or look confused, and what they say when it turns green and speaks.

## Write down (voice note is fine)

- Their trade (fabric, phones, food, POS agent), area, phone model
- Their answers, in their words
- What confused them in the demo
- Whether they'd try it, and what would stop them
- Whether you may quote them, and as what ("a fabric seller in Iwo")

Send me the voice notes or photos of your notes after each visit, or all at the end. I write them up in docs/TRADERS.md.
